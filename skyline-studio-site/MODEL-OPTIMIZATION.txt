BMW M2 CS — browser optimization report

Original GLB: 14.03 MB
Optimized GLB: 8.45 MB
Optimized GZIP download: 5.24 MB

Preserved:
- 39 meshes
- 275,844 triangles
- 33 embedded textures
- all materials and model nodes

Optimized:
- 39 index buffers: uint32 -> uint16
- 39 position accessors: float32 -> normalized uint16 with per-mesh decode transforms
- 39 normal accessors: float32 -> normalized int16
- 17 tangent accessors: float32 -> normalized int16
- 27 UV accessors: float32 -> normalized uint16 when values are in [0,1]
- 4 RGB base-color/emissive images changed to high-quality JPEG where smaller
- one 1024px emissive map reduced to 512px
- normal/roughness/metalness data maps kept lossless
- gzip compression level 9
- mobile renderer pixel ratio capped at 1.0
- direct streaming gzip decompression in the browser

Measured max reconstruction errors:
- position: 0.00003515 source-local units
- normal angle: 0.0450 degrees
- tangent angle: 0.0275 degrees

No triangle decimation was performed, so small exterior and interior details are retained.
